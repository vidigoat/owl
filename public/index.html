    Why this is different from a photo app

    It won't guess with your money

    A diagnosis has to be upheld against that model's service data before you are asked to spend anything. When three causes sit too close together, it says so and books a technician instead.

    Your hands are already busy

    You cannot hold a phone inside a dishwasher. Every step is spoken, paced to what you are doing, and the screen shows one instruction at a time from across the room.

    It finishes the job

    The exact part for your model number, ordered in one word through Amazon Wallet — then it checks the panel afterwards to confirm the fault actually cleared.
