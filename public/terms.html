Terms of use

Owl is a project built for the Amazon Developer Hackathon, offered as it is, free.

It is not a technician

It reads a machine's own service information and tells you what that says. It can be wrong, a manual can be wrong, and a machine can have a fault no manual describes. Stop if anything you are asked to do seems unsafe, and call a qualified technician.

Mains electricity

It will not guide any repair behind mains wiring, and neither should you attempt one. Switch the machine off at the wall and unplug it before you open anything.

Ordering

Ordering is simulated. Nothing is charged and nothing is shipped.

No warranty

It is provided without warranty of any kind. You use it at your own risk, and the people who built it are not liable for damage to your appliance, your home or you.

Manufacturers' material

Quotations from manuals are short, attributed to their manufacturer and cited to the page they are printed on. The manuals themselves belong to their manufacturers and are not redistributed here.
