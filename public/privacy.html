Privacy

Second Set of Hands is a project built for the Amazon Developer Hackathon. This page says what it does with what you give it, and nothing it does not do.

No accounts

There is nothing to sign up for. It never asks for your name, email address, phone number or home address.

What it remembers

So the second conversation about a machine is shorter than the first, it keeps a household record: the appliances you mention by model number, and the repairs you tell it were done, with dates. That record is a file on whichever server runs the add-on. On the public demo there is nowhere durable to write it, so it is held in memory for the conversation and written nowhere — the add-on says so when it notes a repair — and it is not tied to you in any way it could be traced back.

What goes to Amazon Bedrock

When you show it a photo of a control panel, a rating plate or a manual page, the image is sent to Amazon Bedrock (Amazon Nova) to be read. When you talk to the live agent, what you say and the tools' answers are sent to Amazon Bedrock so the model can choose what to do next. This project does not keep the photos. A live conversation is held in memory while you are using it and dropped after 30 idle minutes.

Orders

Ordering is simulated. No payment is taken, no card is charged, and no part is shipped: the checkout exists to show that it will only offer a part once the diagnosis is verified and you have heard the total.

Nothing else

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking cookies, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone.
